# General Policy Management

This section covers the API endpoints for managing general email policies.

## Overview

General policy endpoints allow you to configure system-wide email policies that apply to all users and domains. These policies control general email behavior and system settings.

## Available Operations

- Create general policies
- List existing general policies
- Update policy settings
- Delete general policies
- Configure system-wide email rules

## Authentication

All general policy management endpoints require authentication. Please refer to the [Authentication](/docs/authentication) section for details on how to authenticate your requests.

## Permissions

Appropriate permissions are required to perform general policy management operations. See the [Permissions](/docs/permissions) section for more information.
